Digital health AI

Foundation-model LVEF assessment from portable single-lead ECGs (DECAAF II)

An end-to-end pipeline that combines frozen ECGFounder embeddings, repeated-recording pooling, multitask prediction, and clinical covariates to detect reduced left ventricular ejection fraction after AF ablation.

  • Repeated patient-level nested cross-validation, cross-fitted stacking, and calibration.
  • Sensitivity analyses across cardiac rhythm, recording windows, and clinical covariates.
  • Echo-prioritization rules and decision-curve analysis; locked pipeline applied to 15 external patients (exploratory).
AUROC 0.908 · LVEF ≤40% AUROC 0.857 · LVEF <50% MAE 5.09 pts n = 359

Manuscript and HRS abstract in preparation.

Wearable PPG and incident heart failure

Linked longitudinal wearable PPG recordings with EHR follow-up to study physiological change before heart-failure onset.

  • Signal-quality and pulse-interval feature workflows at 25/100 Hz (pulse rate, interval variability, RMSSD, sample entropy).
  • Diagnosed timestamp artifacts; designed within-person pre/post-HF comparisons.
PPGSignal processingEHR linkage

MRI–ECG cross-modal learning for atrial substrate

Contrastive alignment of ECG and cardiac MRI representations for post-ablation fibrosis and left-atrial remodeling.

  • Patient-held-out evaluation with probes for leakage and patient-identity shortcuts.

A counting-process framework for causal effects on disease onset, death before onset, and post-onset death, using weighted entry balance.

  • Applied to REACHnet comparative-safety data.
